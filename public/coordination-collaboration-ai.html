Challenge

AI is accelerating delivery. Coordination is struggling to keep up.

AI helps individuals move faster. The challenge is that organisations often struggle to absorb that increased speed. As teams generate more ideas, decisions, code, and experiments, leaders face more dependencies, more coordination overhead, and more stakeholder alignment challenges.

More parallel work across multiple teams
Faster decisions with less time to align
More stakeholders with competing priorities
Increased organisational coordination overhead
More dependencies and handoffs between teams
More conflict around priorities and trade-offs

Outcomes

What you'll leave with

Have the conversations your team keeps avoiding

Learn to distinguish useful friction from conflict that needs resolving, practise feedback with cohort peers, and develop a personal playbook for conversations that currently go sideways.

Stop the endless alignment loops slowing your team down

Identify where decisions get stuck, practise moving groups from debate to closure, and leave with a decision protocol you can use in your next planning cycle.

Fix the team structure that's creating more work than it solves

Map unnecessary handoffs and dependencies, identify skill gaps, and choose coordination patterns that fit your organisational context.

Keep your stakeholders aligned without it becoming a second job

Work out which stakeholders need what and how often, prevent misalignment from turning into escalation, and build a practical stakeholder cadence.

Test your thinking against people doing the same work

Bring real challenges into structured peer sessions, get direct coaching from Giuseppe and Simon, and build relationships with leaders navigating similar problems.

Turn what you learn into changes at work

Take stock of where coordination is costing you most, choose the changes worth trying first, and leave with concrete next steps you can put into practice immediately.
