Influence and Alignment Across Organisational Boundaries

Navigate stakeholder relationships, manage expectations, and create alignment without relying on formal authority.

One-day live course Limited to 16 participants Interactive workshops Online delivery

Challenge

Important work stalls when stakeholders have different priorities, expectations, and levels of influence.

Stakeholder management is not about keeping everyone happy. It is about understanding the landscape around your work, communicating in ways that matter to different people, and creating enough alignment to move forward.

Important stakeholders are involved too late
Expectations differ without being made explicit
The same message lands differently with different audiences
You depend on people you have no authority over
Upward communication gets lost in too much detail
Stakeholder conversations happen reactively instead of deliberately

Outcomes

What you'll leave with

Map the stakeholder landscape

Identify the people and groups who influence your work and understand their interests, expectations, and relationships.

Adapt communication to the audience

Translate complexity and frame information around what different stakeholders need to understand or decide.

Influence without formal authority

Use relationships, credibility, framing, and involvement to create movement across organisational boundaries.

Manage expectations and alignment

Make expectations explicit, address differences early, and build more deliberate stakeholder interactions.

Curriculum

Curriculum

Learn to map the stakeholders around your work, understand their interests, expectations, and relationships, and identify where alignment is missing. Build a stakeholder map for a real initiative from your own work.

Practise adapting your message to different audiences, influencing people without formal authority, and making expectations explicit. Leave with a stakeholder engagement plan you can use immediately.
