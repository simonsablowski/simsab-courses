Improve collaboration across teams and functions, reduce coordination friction, and manage dependencies more effectively.

Challenge

Cross-functional work breaks down when responsibilities, dependencies, and coordination mechanisms do not match the work.

Collaboration across teams is not solved by adding more meetings. Effective cross-functional work requires clarity about ownership, deliberate interfaces between teams, and coordination mechanisms that fit the dependencies involved.

Work repeatedly waits at team or functional boundaries
Dependencies are discovered too late
Ownership is unclear when work spans several teams
Coordination relies on a few key individuals
Teams optimise locally while the wider work slows down
More meetings are added instead of fixing the underlying interaction

Curriculum

Curriculum

Learn to map the boundaries, hand-offs, and dependencies involved in a piece of work, and identify where and why collaboration breaks down. Apply the analysis to real cross-team work from your own organisation.

Practise clarifying ownership, choosing coordination mechanisms that fit the dependencies, and removing unnecessary interfaces. Leave with concrete changes you can propose to the teams involved.
