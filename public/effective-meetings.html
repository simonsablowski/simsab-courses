Design and facilitate meetings that stay focused, involve the right people, and turn discussion into decisions and action.

Challenge

Too many meetings consume time without moving the work forward.

Meetings become expensive when their purpose is unclear, the wrong people are involved, or discussion never turns into a decision. Effective meetings are designed deliberately: with a clear goal, the right participants, a format that fits the task, and a process that helps the group move forward.

Meetings without a clear purpose or outcome
Too many people involved in every discussion
Conversations dominated by a few voices
Discussions that drift or repeatedly run over time
Decisions that get discussed but never close
Recurring meetings that are no longer useful

Outcomes

What you'll leave with

Know when you actually need a meeting

Clarify the purpose and desired outcome, and recognise when an asynchronous alternative would work better.

Design meetings around the right people and format

Invite the people who genuinely need to be there and choose a structure that fits the conversation.

Facilitate focused discussions and decisions

Use practical techniques to involve different voices, keep discussions on track, navigate disagreement, and help groups reach decisions.

Improve the meetings you already have

Apply what you learn to a real meeting from your own work and leave with concrete changes you can put into practice immediately.

Curriculum

Curriculum

Learn how to define a clear purpose and outcome, decide who needs to participate, and choose a format that fits the task. Explore alternatives to meetings when synchronous discussion isn't necessary.

Practise techniques for structuring discussion, involving different voices, managing time and disagreement, and moving groups towards decisions. Apply the techniques by redesigning a real meeting from your own work.
