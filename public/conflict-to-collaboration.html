Challenge

Conflict becomes costly when disagreement turns into avoidance, escalation, or recurring friction.

Workplace conflict is rarely just about the issue people are arguing about. Different interests, assumptions, pressures, and communication patterns shape what happens next. Learning to diagnose conflict before trying to resolve it makes difficult conversations more constructive.

Outcomes

What you'll leave with

Diagnose conflict before trying to solve it

Map the people, positions, interests, dynamics, and underlying tensions shaping a conflict.

Prepare for difficult conversations

Clarify what matters, separate observations from interpretations, and enter conversations with a constructive intent.

Move from positions to interests

Explore what people actually need and identify options that address competing concerns.

Navigate towards resolution

Use practical techniques to de-escalate tension, work through disagreement, and agree useful next steps.
